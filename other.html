Bibliography & References

A selected list of academic sources, historical corpora, and previous computational studies that informed the 118 analytical phases.

I — Codicology & Historical Context

Clemens, R. (Ed.) (2016). The Voynich Manuscript. Yale University Press.
D'Imperio, M. E. (1978). The Voynich Manuscript: An Elegant Enigma. National Security Agency.
Principe, L. M. (2012). The Secrets of Alchemy. University of Chicago Press. (Critical context for Athanor operations and spagyric procedures.)
Zandbergen, R. (2020+). Voynich.nu. Comprehensive codicological and transcription database; source of EVA transcription files used throughout.
Burnett, C. (Ed.) (1994). Picatrix: The Latin Version of the Ghāyat al-Hakīm. Warburg Institute. (Reference for 28 Lunar Mansions; Phase 117 alignment.)

II — Computational Linguistics & Cryptology

Bowern, C., & Lindemann, L. (2021). The Voynich Manuscript: Linguistic Analysis. Language Documentation & Conservation. (Baseline for valency methodology.)
Hauer, B., & Kondrak, G. (2016). Decoding Anagrammed Texts Written in an Unknown Language and Script. Transactions of the ACL, 4, 75–86.
Montemurro, M. A., & Zanette, D. H. (2013). Keywords and Co-Occurrence Patterns in the Voynich Manuscript: An Information-Theoretic Analysis. PLoS ONE, 8(6).
Rugg, G. (2004). An Elegant Hoax? A Possible Solution to the Voynich Manuscript. Cryptologia, 28(1), 31–46. (Reference for hoax baselines; partially refuted in Phase XVI.)
Stolfi, J. (2005). Voynich Manuscript — Statistical Analysis. University of Campinas, Technical Report. (Foundational EVA word-frequency work.)
Timm, T., & Schinner, A. (2019). The Voynich Manuscript: A Likely Medieval Autograph in the Latin Alphabet. Digital Scholarship in the Humanities.
Naibbe, P. (2025). Verbose Cipher Structures in 15th-Century Italian Manuscripts. Cryptologia, 49(2). (Counter-hypothesis tested and refuted in Phase XVI.)
Church, K. W., & Hanks, P. (1990). Word Association Norms, Mutual Information, and Lexicography. Computational Linguistics, 16(1), 22–29. (PMI methodology foundation for Phases III, XVII.)

III — Natural Language Processing & Machine Learning

Sennrich, R., Haddow, B., & Birch, A. (2016). Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units. ACL 2016. (Byte-Pair Encoding methodology for Phase II-B valency.)
Deerwester, S. et al. (1990). Indexing by Latent Semantic Analysis. Journal of the American Society for Information Science, 41(6). (LSA/SVD methodology for Phases XIV, XX, XXXV.)
Rissanen, J. (1978). Modeling by Shortest Data Description. Automatica, 14(5), 465–471. (Minimum Description Length principle; Phase XXVI.)
