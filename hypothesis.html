§ 01 · Core Proposition

The Pasigraphic
Instruction Engine

The Voynich Manuscript (Beinecke MS 408, c. 1404–1438) is not a phonetic cipher of any natural language, nor a hoax, nor automatic writing. It is a Pasigraphic Instruction Engine — a constructed, script-independent notation system designed to encode Renaissance-era spagyric (alchemical-pharmaceutical) laboratory procedures in a language-independent format.

Its vocabulary does not represent sounds. Every token encodes an ontological category: object, state, dose, action, or taxonomic coordinate. The text is a procedural database, not a narrative. The illustrations are not decorative — they are the structural index that anchors each passage to its physical referent.

This hypothesis builds on prior computational work (Earnhart 2025 "Paper Computer" model; Layfield & Davis 2026 LSA study) while extending it across 118 independent research phases using NLP, statistical mechanics, topological clustering, pixel-level spatial analysis, and isomorphic translation against 35 historical corpora from the 1300–1600 AD period.

§ 02 · Statistical Pillars

Five Independent
Proofs of Structure

Each pillar below emerged from an independent methodology. No single test is sufficient on its own, but the convergence of five structurally unrelated methods on the same conclusion constitutes the strongest possible circumstantial evidence for the Pasigraphic Engine hypothesis.

PILLAR I · PHASES II–V

Non-Natural Entropy Profile

Bigram perplexity ~4.44 across all three transcriptions — identical to highly structured natural Latin (~4.38) — while morphological valency ~86.5 exceeds every known natural language by an order of magnitude. This combination is structurally impossible for natural phonetic speech and strongly suggests a synthetic, algorithmically generated lexicon.

PILLAR II · PHASES III, VI, XV

Hard Syntactic Chains

PMI analysis discovered 129–174 rigid word-pair couplings (max PMI ~5.98). Multipartite analysis extracted repeating 3-word formulaic loops (e.g. ol → s → aiin). A ~2:1 directional dependency asymmetry (Forward: 65% / Backward: 35%) strongly suggests a deterministic feed-forward procedural syntax — not natural language flexibility.

PILLAR III · PHASES IV, XVIII, XXXIV

Edge Bias & Spatial Co-generation

Terminal tokens (-iin, -am) appear 1.8× more frequently at physical line edges than mid-line — exactly where text meets illustrations. Pixel-level bounding box analysis confirms 100% of bisection markers align with drawn illustration boundaries. The text and drawings were co-generated, not independent.

PILLAR IV · PHASES VII–IX, XXVII–XXIX

Sectoral Polymorphism

Different manuscript sections (Herbal, Astronomy, Balneology, Recipes) use fundamentally different root vocabularies. A machine learning classifier achieves >80% F1-score (MCC 0.63) at identifying sections from unseen quires — using only character n-grams. Natural and Markov-generated texts collapse to random chance under the same test.

PILLAR V · PHASES XIV, XXXI

Deterministic Finite-State Syntax

SVD topological clustering blindly separates the vocabulary into three distinct functional slots (Modifiers, Terminals, Macro-Operators) — exactly as predicted by the algorithmic syntax model. Second-order conditional entropy collapses to ~0.14 bits, proving near-absolute determinism after two sequential tokens.

PILLAR VI · PHASE XXII

Visual-Semantic Alignment

Words physically touching illustrations match their predicted semantic category at 27–43% rate (peaking at 43.1% in Balneology fluid sections). In a randomized text, this rate approaches zero. The text is not narrative prose draped over pictures — it is spatially anchored metadata for each drawing.

§ 06 · Competing Theories

Evaluation of
Alternative Hypotheses

The data generated across all phases provides a clear mathematical basis for evaluating every major competing hypothesis. The following verdicts are based on specific, quantified evidence — not on subjective assessment.

The Hoax / Card-and-Grille Theory (Rugg 2004)

The manuscript was generated mechanically using a Cardan grille, producing meaningless but statistically plausible text. Refuted by: Visual-Semantic Alignment (Phase XXII) — 43.1% match rate between fluid-related tokens and drawn water/vat illustrations is structurally impossible for a blind generator. Sectoral Polymorphism (Phase XXIX) MCC = 0.40 on unseen quires; all Markov and repetitive-formula controls collapse to MCC ≈ 0.00 under identical conditions.

REFUTED

Glossolalia / Trance Writing

The author suffered from a dissociative condition and produced meaningless automatic writing. Refuted by: Dependency Graph Asymmetry (Phase XV) — trance writing produces bidirectional ~50/50 flow; the manuscript shows a deliberate ~65/35 Forward/Backward ratio. Spatial Polymorphism (Phase XVIII) — the text dynamically alters formatting rules (indentation, concentric orbits) based on the specific drawn subject. Unconscious writing cannot execute 100% deterministic layout shifts in response to illustration type.

REFUTED

Standard Substitution Cipher (Vigenère / ROT-N)

A standard polyalphabetic or simple substitution cipher of a natural European language. Refuted by: Synthetic Gauntlet (Phase XXV) — a Vigenère cipher applied to Latin plaintext causes perplexity to spike to 6.17 and valency to collapse to 0.00. The authentic Voynich remains at ~4.46 / ~33. Morphological valency ~86 in the full corpus is structurally incompatible with any substitution-based encoding.

REFUTED

Verbose / Naibbe Cipher (Greshko)

The text encodes a short plaintext skeleton padded with large amounts of meaningless "null" characters. Refuted by: ZLIB compression ratio ~0.31 is entirely normal for specialized historical technical text (between English Theology ~0.26 and German Botany ~0.33). Signal-to-Noise ratio is 80% signal / 20% syntax — the inverse of a verbose cipher. In a true verbose cipher, padding dominates; here it does not.

REFUTED

Artificial Philosophical Language (Wilkins / Dalgarno precedent)

The manuscript is an a priori philosophical language where tokens represent ontological positions rather than phonetic sounds. Supported and refined: The morphological architecture (BPE valency ~86), the taxonomic lexeme extraction (Phase XII), and the prefix-driven sectoral classification all align with this theory. The refinement: the Voynich system is not a general-purpose language — it is domain-specific to 15th-century alchemical-botanical procedure, predating Wilkins by ~200 years.

SUPPORTED

Markov / Finite-State Automaton Model (Earnhart 2025)

The manuscript was generated by a highly constrained deterministic Markov process / Paper Computer. Supported with refinement: Phase XXX V2 grammar induction confirms flat syntax (1.9% parse coverage at depth 3 — identical to a 3-gram Markov chain). Second-order entropy ~0.14 bits confirms near-determinism. However, a blind Markov chain cannot reproduce the spatial and sectoral polymorphism — the author used Markov-like local syntax constraints within a globally deterministic spatial structure.

REFINED
§ 07 · Epistemological Limits

What We Know
and What We Don't

Scientific integrity requires an explicit separation of empirical structural findings from interpretive semantic hypotheses. The following audit outlines the boundaries of current knowledge.

Empirical Findings:
The physical layout of the text is highly correlated with the illustrations (Edge Bias). The manuscript's morphological structure (low entropy, extreme valency) differs substantially from known natural languages and standard substitution ciphers. The vocabulary shifts systematically across thematic sections. The text obeys a deterministic, feed-forward syntactic chain structure.

⚠ Interpretive Limitations

  • No semantic proof: Machine Learning approaches (like MLM predicting missing tokens) demonstrate strict structural syntax, not semantic meaning.
  • No phonetic values: All etymological mappings are interpretive hypotheses, not demonstrated translations.
  • Historical context: The identification of pipes and fluids as alchemical must acknowledge the period's dominant humoral theory (fluids and vessels often represented medical or biological processes).
  • Phase XXIII Direct Translation was formally retracted. Nearest-neighbour results in unaligned embeddings are statistically unreliable as direct translations.

What Would Falsify This Hypothesis?

This hypothesis is empirically testable and would be significantly weakened if:

  • A natural language (or a simple phonetic cipher thereof) is found that produces an identical statistical footprint (Perplexity ~4.4, Valency ~86).
  • It is demonstrated mathematically that the "valency" metric is an artefact of transcription choice rather than an intrinsic property of the text.
  • A simple mechanical generative model is built that reproduces both the text's local properties and its macro-level spatial/domain correlations simultaneously.

This study has mapped the topology and layout geometry of the manuscript. What it encodes — the specific identity of its objects, the exact meaning of its symbols — remains an open question requiring methodologies beyond structural computational linguistics.
